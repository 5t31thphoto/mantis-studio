MANTIS STUDIO

A pocket music workstation for M5Stack Core2

Make music without a studio-sized interface.

MANTIS STUDIO is a four-track embedded groovebox: drum pads, lead synth, bass synth and an audio recorder, all locked to one musical timeline.

Perform, overdub, quantize or leave it loose. No piano-roll maze. No deep menus.

Tracks

DRUM · four-pad performance and loop recording
LEAD · chord, scale and arpeggio synth
BASS · electro, chip and dub voices
AUDIO · microphone / instrument recording to SD
MIX · four-track levels, mute, solo and transport

Install

Use Chrome or Edge with a USB data cable. The device firmware is self-contained and does not require an internet connection after flashing.
