MANTIS STUDIO

a tiny, touch-first music workstation for the M5Stack Core2 — drums, lead, bass, an audio track, a mixer, and a mantis that dances to your song

Install

Plug the Core2 in with a data USB-C cable, use Chrome or Edge on a computer, and press the button.

Your browser can't flash devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop.

Download firmware-merged.bin (flash at offset 0x0).

Everywhere

A / C change screen. B is the context button: it records when something is armed, otherwise it plays and stops. Hold B for tap tempo with an audible click. The header flashes on every beat, with bar.beat, four beat dots and the loop bar, so the pulse is always visible. REC in the header chooses whether your playing is recorded into the loop; hold CLR for a second to wipe the track.

Screens

DRUM
Four pads: hat, open hat, kick, snare. Play them while the loop runs and they record, quantised. Hold a pad for a second to arm it, then press B to sample your own sound into it.
LEAD
Sixteen scale-locked keys, two fingers at once, slide between notes. Key, scale, note / chord / arp, and pluck / pad / chip sounds. Tilt the Core2 to open and close the filter.
BASS
A mono bass with glide: electro squelch, chip or dub. Same scale and key as the lead, so everything fits.
AUDIO
Record 1, 2 or 4 bars of voice or anything else, locked to the start of the loop. Tap the red button, then B.
MIX
Faders and meters for every track and the master, tap a name to mute, hold it to clear. Save and load four projects on the SD card.
MANTIS
The mantis dances to your song, locked to the beat. Tap to pet it.

Good to know

The Core2's microphone and speaker share one audio port, so while you record a sample or a clip the speaker goes quiet. You get a count-in, and the Core2 buzzes on every beat (harder on the one) so you can feel the tempo. Playback resumes from bar 1 when you're done.

Projects live on the SD card at /MANTIS/PROJECTS/<slot>/. Optional replacement kit sounds go in /MANTIS/SAMPLES/ as 16 kHz mono 16-bit raw files: kick.raw, snare.raw, hat_closed.raw, hat_open.raw.
